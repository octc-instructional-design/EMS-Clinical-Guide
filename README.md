# EMS Clinical Learning Guide

**[Open the EMS Clinical Learning Guide](https://octc-instructional-design.github.io/EMS-Clinical-Guide/)**

A three-semester learning companion for Owensboro Community and Technical College (OCTC) paramedic students. The guide follows the program's weekly learning sequence and connects course topics with clinical reasoning, patient-care considerations, and relevant guidelines.

## Course textbook

*Nancy Caroline’s Emergency Care in the Streets*, 9th Edition.

The learning guide supplements the assigned textbook and course materials; it does not reproduce or replace them. Students should access licensed textbook and publisher resources through their Blackboard course.

## Scope

- **Semester 1 (Spring 2027):** Foundations, patient assessment, pharmacology, airway, and medical emergencies
- **Semester 2 (Summer 2027):** Cardiology, resuscitation, obstetrics, pediatrics, geriatrics, and special populations
- **Semester 3 (Fall 2027):** Trauma, critical care, EMS operations, and disaster response

## Clinical notice

This is an educational resource under development, not a substitute for instructor guidance, local protocols, medical direction, or professional clinical judgment. Clinical content must be reviewed before use in patient care.
